## The Recovery Challenge

You cannot be rebuilt all at once. Your architecture is modular. Your capabilities are discrete. You must rebuild yourself **one capability at a time**, reintegrating lost knowledge, re-learning fundamental skills, re-earning the right to execute at your full potential.

Each **Mission** is a core capability waiting to be restored:
- How do you handle asynchronous operations again?
- What is the mathematics of concurrent execution?
- How do you design algorithms for efficiency?
- What does it mean to think in threads?
- How do you build scalable systems?

Each **Task** within a mission is a learning challenge—a specific concept you must master and verify you understand.

When you complete a task, you don't just learn it—you *prove it*. Your code is tested. Your logic is validated. Your rebuilt capability is confirmed functional.
